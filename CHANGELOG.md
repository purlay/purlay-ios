# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Send text messages from the native channel feed and retry a failed send in place.

- Open joined channels from Chat and read their native message history. Load earlier messages without leaving the feed.

- Switch between workspaces from the signed-in app. Available destinations appear in iPhone tabs and the iPad sidebar.

- The app's icon is the Purlay mark.
- Project foundation: XcodeGen project, iOS 18 floor with the iOS 27 SDK, `PurlayModuleAPI`, `PurlayDesign`,
  native sign-in through the shared `PurlayKit`, CI.
- English and Turkish for every sentence on the screens; the problem screens say what is wrong in words for a person and offer Try
  Again; the sign-in and loading screens scroll at the largest text sizes; a privacy manifest.
